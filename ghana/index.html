Why Ghana

Africa's leading gold jurisdiction

Ghana has established itself as one of the world's most attractive gold exploration destinations. International junior exploration companies continue to acquire, option, farm into, and joint venture highly prospective licences across the country. The opportunity remains significant — the challenge is knowing where to focus.

  • Long and well-documented mining history
  • Stable mining legislation and an established permitting framework
  • Proven world-class gold belts
  • Existing mining infrastructure
  • Multiple producing mines and development projects
  • An active exploration and transaction market
Market context Many market participants continue to view gold as a strategic long-term asset amid global monetary uncertainty. In that environment, well-positioned ground in an established jurisdiction can be among the most contested assets in the sector.
